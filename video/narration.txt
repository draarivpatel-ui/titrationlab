TitrationLab is an offline acid base titration workbench. It runs as one static page with no accounts, no network calls, and no hidden services.

It does not draw a canned curve. It solves charge balance at every volume, brackets the root on a pH grid, refines it by bisection, and keeps water autoionization in the model.

The strong acid preset shows the sharp rise through equivalence. The readout gives the equivalence volume, solved equivalence pH, and charge residual.

The acetic acid preset reveals the buffer region. The curve, half equivalence point, and pKa line up, while phenolphthalein matches the basic equivalence point.

Manual inputs change concentration, volume, Ka, resolution, and indicator. Validation rejects impossible chemistry before recalculation.

The meter table separates exact model pH from a two decimal classroom reading. The curve can also download as CSV data.

TitrationLab is honest about its limits. It assumes 25 degrees Celsius, ignores activity coefficients, and models one monoprotic acid. It is a transparent teaching simulation, not instrument grade measurement.
