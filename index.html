    The chemistry inside

    Every plotted point solves charge balance by bracketing the root on a pH grid and refining it by bisection.

    Charge balance

    [H+] + [B+] = [OH-] + [A-]

    Positive ions balance negative ions after mixing. The bisection solve finds the one [H+] that satisfies this equation.

    Weak-acid equilibrium

    Ka = [H+][A-] / [HA]

    Mass balance and Ka partition the weak acid between HA and A- at every titrant volume.

    Equivalence point

    Veq = CaVa / Cb

    The equivalence pH is solved from the resulting solution composition. It is never forced to pH 7.
